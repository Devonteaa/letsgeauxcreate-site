FAQ

How long does a custom rug take?

Typical turnaround is 2–4 weeks depending on size and design complexity. Rush options available.

What yarns and backing do you use?

Premium acrylic/wool blends with non-slip felt or rubberized backing. We’ll recommend the best fit for your use case.

Do you ship?

Yes—tracked shipping across the U.S. Local pickup coming when the studio opens.

Can you replicate logos or characters?

We can work with your original art or licensed marks. For trademarked characters/logos, buyer must have rights or we’ll pivot the design.
